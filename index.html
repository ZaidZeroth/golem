FIELD NOTES

Try NOT + RED, NOT + HAND, HAND + HAND, or build a sense/action expression.

Suggested constructions in this draft: NOT RED, FIST, COMBINE, NOT FIST, LEFT, double-NOT RIGHT, directed hands, sight filters, WHILE senses, DO actions, AND/OR sense pairs, IN ME queries, and sense/action GOLEM pairs. These are provisional readings only: patterns do not match blocks and WHILE/DO do not activate game behavior.

Prototype boundary: combining two squares consumes the dropped square and keeps the target square in place. The exact target-then-dropped tree is retained even when its face label is normalized. No creature simulation, movement, pattern matching, eye closing, or SPLIT/undo history is implemented.
